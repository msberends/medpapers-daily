PubMed author identification
PubMed disambiguates authors with the same name using its Computed Authors algorithm, seeded by a PMID of a paper known to belong to this person. The Search online button above is the easiest way to find and confirm this. To find it manually: open PubMed, search for the author, and click their name in any result — the URL will contain cauthor_id=XXXXXXXX. Authors with common names or who publish across multiple research areas may appear in more than one cluster; enter all relevant IDs separated by commas. If left blank, the app falls back to a plain name search, which may include papers by other people with the same name.
The author’s 16-digit ORCID iD (e.g. 0000-0001-2345-6789). When provided, ORCID is used alongside the seed PMIDs for disambiguation, and is also used during cron fetching as a stable, name-independent identifier. Not all PubMed records include ORCID — seed PMIDs remain the primary source of disambiguation.
