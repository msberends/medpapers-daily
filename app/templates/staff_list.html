How the staff publication tracker works

Add each staff member with their PubMed author name and initials. The system searches PubMed for their papers using the Search online tool (which uses PubMed's computed-author disambiguation) or a set of seed PMIDs you provide. Candidate papers are stored as pending until you review them; reviewed papers become confirmed or probable and feed the publications report.

Workflow

  1. Add a staff member with their last name, initials, and optionally an ORCID or seed PMID(s).
  2. Open their profile and click Search online to find a representative paper, then use it as a seed to pull their full publication cluster from PubMed.
  3. Click Review to go through the pending candidates and confirm or reject each one.
  4. Use the Report to export confirmed publications, filtered by year range, group, or journal quartile.

Scores

  • Score — authorship position: first or last author = 2, second or second-last = 1, any other position = 0.5.
  • Quartile-score — Score × (Scopus percentile ÷ 100). Adjusts for journal standing; empty when Scopus data is unavailable.
